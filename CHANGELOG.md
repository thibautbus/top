# Changelog

What changed in each version of The Oracles Project, the newest first. The release page of each version on GitHub carries the same text, under the installation notes.

## 1.4.0 (2026-11-05)

### New

- **Unvisited rooms.** **Display > Picture > Unvisited rooms**: Shown, as before, or Hidden, where the rooms around Link that he has never entered stay dark until he does, as on the game's own map, so that the wider views give nothing away. Where a dark room meets one you can see, a glimpse of it shows along their border, with whoever happens to stand there, and fades into the dark in a soft, wavy edge rather than a straight cut (the Low quality keeps the straight cut). It reads what your save already knows: an old save shows the rooms you have visited, a new game starts with none, and a room fades in as you first enter it. It works in Enhanced, for Ages, Seasons and the fan games, and changes from the pause too, when the game resumes. Picture's summary says " · Rooms hidden".
- **How to open the menu.** Each game you start now reminds you, under the status bar once you are in play, which key opens the menu: "L3 opens the menu" with a controller (the button you chose in Controls, or Guide when none is), "Esc opens the menu" with the keyboard, "The pause button opens the menu" by touch on Android, "L3 or Back opens the menu" with a controller there. It follows the device you use, and stops once you have opened the menu with that key, or after ten games. It replaces the one-time notice of the first game started with a controller, so it shows again even if you saw that one. **Reset to defaults** in Controls brings it back.
- **Finding the item hotkeys.** In Ages and Seasons, while the item hotkeys are off, the pause menu says where to turn them on, "Item hotkeys are off · Controls › Item hotkeys", the first three times you open it. In **Controls > Hotkeys**, with them off, the slots are greyed and a sentence under them says what they do.
- **Cheats in the fan games.** Gifts of Kinomi, Moonrise Regalia and Temple of Seasons now have **Cheats** in their menu and their pause menu, as Ages and Seasons do: No damage, Ammo never runs out and Rupees never run out, each fan game with its own, separate from its Oracle's. In Temple of Seasons, Rupees never run out makes every purchase free, and Ammo never runs out keeps its seeds as it keeps its bombs.
- **Saves of games with mods.** **Import save…** now brings the save in for the game with mods too: the save the Mods page's Play uses goes to Backups with the old one, and the next game with mods starts from the save imported. On Android, the Mods page has a new **Export save with mods…**, which copies the save of the game with mods wherever you choose, as **Export save…** does for the usual save.

### Improved

- **Smoother room changes.** With **Continuous transitions**, changing rooms now feels much more like walking or swimming in the open:
  - **Everywhere.** Link no longer stops for a moment as the scroll to the next room ends, walking or swimming, crossing an edge diagonally no longer gives him a double step as the scroll starts, and at a room's corner the next scroll no longer pulls him back.
  - **On foot.** He walks through the scroll as you hold the pad. A tap at a room's edge no longer takes him into the next room: it starts only as he crosses the line between the two. Letting go during the scroll leaves him where he is, without being pushed on; turning back walks him back, and across the line into the room he left; holding a direction across the scroll turns him and walks him that way.
  - **Swimming,** at the surface or on Ages' sea floor. He swims through the scroll as anywhere else: the pad and his strokes speed him up and turn him, and when you let go he glides and slows down as in the middle of a room, so a tap at a room's edge no longer pushes him on into the next room; turning back swims him back.
  - **Corners.** Where four rooms meet, a diagonal takes him straight on into the room across without slowing down, on foot or swimming, on Ages' sea floor and in Subrosia too.
  - **Rooms that change as they load.** In Ages and Seasons, this now holds into the rooms that set a few of their own tiles as they load (a door opened, a bridge built, a vine grown), where he used to slow down across.
  - **Back where the game puts him.** A whirlpool, a hole or deep water just past a room's edge now brings him back where the game would, not right next to it again.
  - **In the fan games.** On foot it works in Gifts of Kinomi, Moonrise Regalia and Temple of Seasons too, swimming in Gifts of Kinomi and Temple of Seasons. Holding a diagonal into the next room no longer holds him back while that room loads and then makes him catch up: he goes on sideways through the scroll, stopped only by a wall. A few rooms still make him wait there for now: those that set some of their own tiles as they load, and in Temple of Seasons, some rooms of Holodrum whose ground changes with the season, when he comes in from a room of the same kind.
- **Swimming: Hold in the fan games.** **Controls > Swimming: Hold** now works in Gifts of Kinomi and Temple of Seasons too, as in Ages and Seasons: A held strokes the flippers as fast as tapping.
- **Wide text box and Text size in the fan games.** **Display > Text > Text box** and **Text size** now work in Gifts of Kinomi, Moonrise Regalia and Temple of Seasons too, as in Ages and Seasons: the wide box with its longer lines, in Classic and Compact, and the text box drawn larger where the view has room for it, which changes at once from the pause.
- **Camera.** In Enhanced, the camera no longer sways on stairs and settles at once when Link stops: it follows him at his own speed instead of rocking around it, and with the Smooth camera it sees at once that he has stopped. When the game puts Link back elsewhere, after a fall or a whirlpool, the camera finds him again quickly instead of drifting there, and it no longer runs ahead into a neighbouring room still being prepared. Savestates taken before keep their camera.
- **Fewer black corners.** In Enhanced's wider views, the rooms at the far corners of the view are prepared before a room change brings them into sight, instead of staying black for a moment after it. In Ages' sea, the rooms Link finds after a dive or on coming back up are ready sooner.
- **No black room when one changes.** In Enhanced, a room beside Link that changes while you play, a door that opens or a passage that clears, keeps its picture until the new one is ready instead of going black for a moment, and a room at a corner of the drawn-back view no longer blinks black again and again while Link stands nearby (seen near the Maku Tree in Ages).
- **Savestates taken with other settings.** **Load state** in the pause menu now loads a savestate taken with other Display settings, such as another core, view or text, as one made with an older version may be: the game starts again with the settings it needs, changing as few as it can among Profile, Core, Continuous transitions, Text, Text box, View, Far size and Screen margin, and they stay chosen in Display. A line over the game says what changed, such as "Display set to Fast (mGBA) and Near to load this savestate". A savestate that differs in anything else, such as its language or its mods, stays refused, your game and settings untouched. Where one is refused, the menu now says which choice of Display loads it, for its core, its continuous transitions, the profile or the view's size as already for its text: one taken in Far on a 1280x960 or 1240x1080 screen before Far took a size made for it says "choose Fixed in Display › Picture › Far size from the home screen", one taken on the other core names it in **Display > Advanced > Core**, and one taken before a **Screen margin** changed Far's size names the margin. **F7** now shows over the game why a savestate is refused, and points to **Load state** when that would load it.
- **The hotbar in the game's own look.** In Medium and Far, each item hotkey's slot is drawn like the game's B and A: the key's letter, then the item between the status bar's brackets, placed as on B and A with its count or level (bombs, seeds, a sword's level); Near keeps the compact slots.
- **Hotbar icons from the start.** Each slot of the item hotkeys' hotbar shows its item's icon as soon as the game starts, instead of its name until the item had been on B or A once.
- **Equip sound for the item hotkeys.** Taking an item into a hotkey's slot, in play or from the open inventory, and equipping one with the hotkeys' equip mode, play the game's own equip sound, as the inventory does. Using an item through its hotkey stays silent.

### Fixed

- **The camera after a scene.** In Enhanced, when the game moves Link while the world is not shown, during a scene on the game's own screen, a menu or a fade to white or black, the camera is now already on him when the world comes back, instead of gliding over to him. With the Smooth camera, the picture no longer shifts by itself as a scene starts or ends while Link stands still, and with either camera it no longer glides over to him after a white flash. When the world comes back from a fade, the camera is also already where it stays while Link stands still, instead of drifting slightly a moment later.
- **Continuing by the Maku Tree.** In Enhanced, continuing a game that starts again by the Maku Tree now shows the tree's screen in its place, on its own, as soon as the screen opens, instead of framing it on Link with the screens beside it and then shifting the whole picture once the game had appeared.
- **A scene's walk into the next room.** With **Continuous transitions**, when the game itself walks Link into the next room during a scene, the room now changes as the game has it, so that the scene goes on from where the game places him: early in Ages, he no longer ends up overlapping the character who comes to meet him.
- **Through a room's edge.** With **Continuous transitions**, Link no longer stops for a moment and then jumps ahead as the scroll to the next room starts, on foot with the Pixel camera as when swimming. Swimming, he is also no longer cut in half on a room's left edge just after a scroll, and no longer takes his walking pose for a moment, out of the water, when a scroll left him in the last rows at the bottom of a room.
- **Stuck in a wall after a room change.** With **Continuous transitions**, Link on foot no longer ends up inside a wall of the next room, stuck there while you hold the pad, after crossing a room's edge or corner along a wall: he now stops against it as he would walking in the open.
- **Crossing a room's corner.** With **Continuous transitions**, Link no longer vanishes for a moment where the scroll into the room across follows the first at a corner of four rooms, and with the Smooth camera he no longer slips back slightly as a scroll starts. Carried past the edge of the game's own screen at a corner, he no longer trembles back and forth before settling, nor shows cut in half or vanishes for a moment between two scrolls, and he no longer twitches near the top of a room as a scroll carries him there.
- **Diagonals in dungeons.** With the Smooth camera, Link no longer shakes as he walks diagonally through a dungeon's large rooms, stepping across then down in turn: he glides there as he does outdoors, in every view.
- **Older savestates in a room change.** A savestate made before 1.2.0 with **Continuous transitions**, just as a room change began, now takes Link all the way into the next room when it loads, instead of half way, cut by the screen's edge.
- **Sprites behind the scenery beyond the game's screen.** In Enhanced, what is drawn beyond the game's own screen, Link carried past its edge as a room changes, the people and enemies of a large room, now goes behind the parts of the scenery that hide it on the game's screen (a bridge, an arch, the top of a wall) instead of over them, and a large room no longer shows its people for a moment over the blank room as it loads.
- **People who left a room.** In Enhanced, in Ages and Seasons, a room seen beside the one Link is in no longer shows people who have left it since it was prepared, nor people the game brings in only when Link comes from another side: when the story changes who stands in a room, the room around shows the change the next time you see it, and shows them again if they come back later. It was seen in Ages near the start of the game.
- **Things that flicker beside Link.** In Enhanced, something that flickers, such as a time portal in Ages or the spray of a fountain in Seasons, now moves and flickers in a room seen beside Link as it does in his own room, instead of standing still in a pose that changed with the room he was in.
- **Very large from the pause.** In Medium and Far, changing **Text size** to or from Very large in the pause now shows the dialogue box at its new size behind the menu at once, instead of when the game resumes.
- **Far with a screen margin.** On a screen where Far takes a size made for it (1280x960, 1240x1080), a **Screen margin** no longer leaves a smaller picture than before: Far's size is now chosen for the part of the screen the margin leaves, and Far keeps its usual size where the one made for the screen no longer fits there. A margin changed in the pause changes Far's size from the next game you start.
- **Throwing with the item hotkeys.** When Link holds a bomb, or something he lifted with the power bracelet, pressing a hotkey again now throws it, as B does, instead of doing nothing while the bomb goes off in his hands. Any hotkey throws what he holds, in Ages and Seasons.
- **Item hotkeys during a scene.** In Ages and Seasons, the item hotkeys no longer act in the last moments of a scene that walks Link on its own, when the game still holds the controls.
- **The hotbar in the framed view.** With the item hotkeys on, the hotbar no longer appears for a moment as Link changes rooms while **F3** shows the game's own screen, as in Faithful.
- **Import save.** The message after an import now names each file that went to Backups, the savestate included. On Android, the confirmation shows the folder the save comes from (Download, or the folder you picked it in) instead of a blank line, and with **Import save…** highlighted, the note under Cartridge's save speaks of the import instead of repeating Export's.
- **A fan game's patch on another fan game's page.** Choosing or dropping the patch of another of these fan games, in the version the launcher recognizes (Moonrise Regalia 1.0.6's on Gifts of Kinomi's page, for instance), is now refused, naming the game it is for, instead of being taken as an unrecognized patch playable in Faithful only.
- **A refused patch on the home screen.** A fan game whose patch is there but refused, such as another fan game's, now shows "Patch refused" on the home screen and in the list of fan games, instead of "Patch not found".
- **Two notes in the launcher.** The note under **Controls > Item hotkeys** said that loading a savestate stopped the hotkeys until the next game; it now says what happens: an exchange under way is dropped, and the hotkeys keep working. Display's description of Enhanced no longer names the smooth camera, a choice of its own in **Display > Advanced > Camera**: "The wide world, drawn back, with the rooms around and continuous transitions."

### Notes

- **Defaults.** Nothing changes until you change it: Unvisited rooms stays Shown, and the item hotkeys stay off.
- **Text settings now shared with the fan games.** The Text box and Text size chosen in Display now apply to Gifts of Kinomi, Moonrise Regalia and Temple of Seasons too: if you chose Wide or Large for Ages or Seasons, the fan games open with it.
- **Saves, settings and savestates.** Installed over 1.3.0, 1.4.0 keeps your saves and `settings.txt`, on Android too. Savestates made with 1.3.0 load and keep their camera; one taken with other Display settings now loads from the pause's **Load state**.

## 1.3.0 (2026-10-25)

### New

- **Compact text.** **Display > Text > Text**: Classic, the game's letters each in a cell of its own, as before, or Compact, where each letter takes its own width, so more words fit on a line and a dialogue takes about a fifth fewer boxes. The texts are laid out again for it and still appear letter by letter at the game's pace, with its sounds. It works in Enhanced, in Ages and Seasons in every language, and in the English of the three fan games.
- **Wide text box.** **Display > Text > Text box**: Standard, the game's, or Wide, a longer box drawn by the port, whose lines hold half as much again: with the compact text, a dialogue takes about a third fewer boxes. The game still pages, paces and asks its questions as it does. It works in Enhanced, in Ages and Seasons.
- **Text size.** **Display > Text > Text size**: Normal, Large (half as big again) or Very large (twice as big), the text box drawn larger where the view has room for it, for small screens and reading from a distance. It stays sharp, in whole screen pixels, and changes at once from the pause too. It works in Enhanced, in Ages and Seasons.
- **Instant text.** **Display > Text > Text speed**: Classic, letter by letter at the speed chosen on the file screen, as before, or Instant, where each line of a dialogue appears whole and every page comes much faster; A still turns the page. Characters' voices and the game's own pauses are kept, and your save's text speed is untouched. It works in Faithful and Enhanced, in Ages, Seasons and the three fan games, and from the pause too.
- **Display > Text.** The four settings have a page of their own, opened from Display's **Text** row, with a preview of the box as it will show in your view, at the size it will take there. A savestate loads only with the Text and the Text box it was taken with.
- **Living neighbouring rooms.** In Enhanced, the people and things in the rooms around Link now move as the game moves them, instead of standing still in the pose the view captured. What the game itself keeps still stays still. The Low quality keeps them still, as before.
- **Wide layout for the status bar.** **Display > HUD**: Wide layout, a third choice beside Full width (still the default) and Original. The game's own status bar is split to the corners of the view: the hearts and the rupees on the left, the item hotkeys then B and A on the right, on the bar's colour. The hearts take a single row where the view is wide enough (Medium and Far). It follows the dungeons' keys, Subrosia's ore and fifteen or sixteen hearts, works in the five games and changes at once from the pause too.
- **Camera.** **Display > Advanced > Camera**: Smooth, the camera moving by fractions of a pixel so that diagonal walks and stairs glide, or Pixel, whole pixels as the game does. It changes at once from the pause too. On Android, Smooth is now the default where the game is shown at three times its size or more; it also works with a Screen margin and the touch controls, and Link no longer shifts slightly when swimming from one room to the next.
- **Settings while you play.** From the pause, **Swimming**, **Item hotkeys**, **Window** and **Vsync** now apply to the game you are playing, without going back to the home screen. What a running game cannot change (the text, the text box, the core) is greyed in the pause, which says to change it from the home screen.
- **Import a save.** **Cartridge > Save > Import save…** brings in a save from another emulator, another device or the European ROM, on every platform and for the five games. It asks before replacing anything, keeps the save it replaces, and its savestate, in a **Backups** folder beside it, and refuses a file that is not a save of Oracle of Ages or Seasons. A save played in German, Italian or Spanish on the European ROM gets its language set to English, which the US game needs to show its texts right; the rest of your game is kept.
- **Zipped ROMs.** **Choose ROM…**, **Choose patch…** and the European ROM now take a `.zip` too, and so do a file dropped on the window and the command line: the ROM or the patch inside is read, nothing is unpacked to disk, and the save goes beside the zip, under its name; a zip of a ROM and its patch serves for both. A zip holding several ROMs, or compressed other than the usual way, and a 7z or RAR archive are refused with the reason.
- **Export a save on Android.** **Cartridge > Save > Export save…** puts a copy of your save where you choose (Downloads, an SD card), to play it elsewhere, without a cable. On a computer, **Open folder** shows it as before. A save made here also works with the European ROM in other emulators, beside that ROM under the same name; Cartridge says so under the save.
- **Display > Picture.** How the game's picture shows now has a page of its own, opened from Display's **Picture** row, which sums up its settings beside it: **Scaling** and **Screen margin**, moved from Advanced, **Color correction**, moved from Display's first page, and two settings that only `settings.txt` held until now, on every platform, Android included. **Menus & scenes**: View or Large, the menus, the map, the cutscenes and the file screen at the view's scale, or alone at their own largest scale, easier to read on a small screen (Large is Android's default); it changes at once from the pause too. **Far size**, on 1280x960 and 1240x1080 screens only, where it changes something: Fitted, Far at a size made for the screen, or Fixed, at its usual size. **Display > Advanced** keeps Core, Continuous transitions, Vsync, Neighbor workers and Camera.
- **About.** The version number at the bottom left of the home screen now opens **About**, with the version and **Update check**: On, or Off for no network request at all, which also hides a newer version's notice. It works on every platform, Android included, where the update check could not be turned off until now.
- **Linux: an AppImage.** Linux now has an AppImage, one file to make executable and run, with its icon and menu entry for the tools that install AppImages. The `.tar.gz` stays, for systems where the AppImage does not start. Both also come for Linux ARM64 (Raspberry Pi 5, Asahi Linux...), experimental: not yet tried on real hardware, feedback welcome.

### Improved

- **Smoother diagonal transitions.** With **Continuous transitions**, crossing a room edge diagonally, walking or swimming, stalls much less: Link now carries on across while the next room loads, instead of being held back. A slight catch can still be felt as the scroll ends. It works in Ages and Seasons.
- **The hotbar through dialogues.** In Enhanced, the hotbar of the **Item hotkeys** now stays with the status bar through dialogues, scrolls and fades, fading and flashing with it, instead of hiding at every text box. It is hidden in cutscenes.

### Fixed

- In Ages' underwater rooms, the underwater dungeon included, and under the sea, the water's ripple no longer shakes the rooms when the camera moves, and it keeps rippling while the screen scrolls to the next room. An interior under the sea no longer sways from side to side with the waves, and with the Smooth camera the scenery now glides around Link as he swims diagonally on the sea floor, as it does on foot, instead of turning at every step.
- In Enhanced, a house no longer moves as Link walks out of it.
- At the end of a fade, the rooms around no longer flash a wrong tint for a moment.
- In the drawn-back view (Medium and Far), the farthest rooms stay black much less often, and fill in much faster, when Link walks into ground he has not been to yet, or after a savestate is loaded beside a room that starts a scene.
- With **Menus & scenes** on Large (the default on Android), a cutscene that ends on a blank screen before the next room now keeps its size through the blank, instead of shrinking for a moment.
- A newer version found while a game was running now shows as soon as you return to the home screen, instead of at the next launch.
- On a device without a web browser (some Android consoles), opening a newer version's page now shows its address instead of doing nothing.
- `settings.txt` keeps the settings this version does not know, which a newer version or a test build wrote there, instead of losing them at each launch. A setting it knows, written with a value it does not know, goes back to its default.
- A setting written by hand with spaces or capitals in `settings.txt`, such as `update_check = OFF`, now applies, instead of being ignored and replaced by the default.

### Notes

- **Defaults.** Nothing changes until you change it: Text Classic, Text box Standard, Text size Normal and Text speed Classic play the game's own text, the status bar stays Full width, and Menus & scenes stays Large on Android and View on computers. On Android, the camera now moves by fractions of a pixel (Smooth) where the game is shown at three times its size or more, on most phones and on the larger handhelds' screens; **Display > Advanced > Camera** chooses.
- **Settings that moved.** **Scaling** and **Screen margin** moved from Display's Advanced page, and **Color correction** from Display's first page, to **Display > Picture**.
- **Saves, settings and savestates.** Installed over 1.2.0, 1.3.0 keeps your saves and `settings.txt`, on Android too. Savestates made with 1.2.0 load and resume where they were taken, as long as Display > Text keeps the Classic text and the Standard box; one taken with the Compact text or the Wide box loads only with the same.

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-18)

Most of this release answers what players asked for after 1.1.0, many of them on Retroid, AYN and Anbernic handhelds. Thank you for every report, screenshot and log, and to everyone who tested the builds and posted feedback: they shaped this version.

### New

- **Languages.** Ages and Seasons can now play in French, German, Italian and Spanish, in the official translations, or in the European edition's English. Choose your European ROM in the game's **Cartridge** page, under **European ROM**, then pick the **Language** row, where each language is listed by its own name. The texts are taken from your European ROM once, on your own device, and nothing of it is shipped. The screens that carry text are translated too: the file select, the save menu, the game over screen and the dungeons' names. Mistakes of the official translations (a place named for another, an item named several ways, a wrong word) are corrected. The title screen and the credits stay as the US game has them. English (US), the default, is the cartridge's own text, untouched.
- **Cheats.** Ages and Seasons have a **Cheats** page, in the game's menu after Mods and in the pause menu: **No damage** (enemies, spikes and falls no longer cost hearts; Link is still knocked back), **Ammo never runs out** (seeds, bombs and Bombchus are not used up) and **Rupees never run out** (buying costs nothing; mods still charge). Cheats play with your usual save, and what you keep stays in it. From the pause, a change applies as soon as you resume.
- **Full-width status bar.** In Enhanced, the status bar's colour now runs across the whole width of the view instead of leaving two dark bands beside it. **Display > HUD**: Full width (the new default) or Original, the bar as the game draws it. It works in the five games and changes at once from the pause.
- **Hold to swim.** **Controls > Swimming**: Tap, as the game wants it, or Hold, where holding a direction swims at full speed, instead of tapping A with the flippers or tapping the direction with Ages' mermaid suit. It works in Ages and Seasons, in Faithful and Enhanced.
- **A controller button for the menu.** The pause menu now opens from a controller without going back to the home screen: the left stick's click (L3) by default, and Guide (the Home button) where the system lets it through. Change the button in **Controls > Menu**: any button the game and the item hotkeys leave free, or a trigger. The first game you start with a controller says once which button it is ("Press L3 for the menu.").
- **Diagonal transitions.** With **Continuous transitions**, walking or swimming diagonally into the next room now carries on diagonally through the scroll, instead of sliding straight along the edge for half a second. Walls still hold Link where the game would. It works in Ages, Seasons and the three fan games.
- **Scaling.** **Display > Advanced > Scaling**: Sharp (the default), the game at its largest whole scale with every pixel exact, as before, or Fill, the image as large as the screen allows in its proportions, the pixels slightly softer. Fill applies in fullscreen. On a screen where the view would otherwise sit small in a black frame (Far 4:3 on a 640x480 handheld), Fill lets it fill the screen.
- **New version notice.** When a newer version is published, the home screen says so next to the version at the bottom left ("1.3.0 available"). Select it (Down from Exit, then Enter or A, or click or tap it) to open its release page in your browser; the launcher stays open. The launcher asks GitHub once at launch, in the background, and sends nothing else; `update_check=off` in `settings.txt` turns this off (not on Android for now, where `settings.txt` is out of reach).
- **Screen margin.** **Display > Advanced > Screen margin**: Off (the default), Auto, Small or Large. For a device whose screen hides its top or bottom edge, cutting off the status bar or the top of the inventory: Auto keeps clear of the notch or curved edge the system reports, Small and Large keep a fixed margin at the top and bottom. It applies at once, from the pause too.

### Improved

- **Far view fitted to the screen.** On 4:3 screens of about 1280 pixels across, Far showed at twice its size in a black frame. It now takes a size made for the screen and fills it at three times: 426x320 on a 1280x960 screen (Retroid Pocket Nova and Mini, Anbernic RG477M) and 412x360 on a 1240x1080 screen (Retroid Pocket Mini V2 and Classic, AYN Thor's lower screen). Display shows the size the game will take, and in the 4:3 layout View's help says "Far fits this screen". Every other screen keeps Far's usual size.
- **Smoother camera on computers.** In Enhanced on Windows, macOS and Linux, the camera now moves by fractions of a pixel at your screen's own resolution, and Link is drawn at his exact place: walking diagonally or on stairs, the scenery glides instead of stepping a pixel at a time, and Link stays steady in it. It shows on a screen at least twice the view's size (Far on a 1080p screen, for instance), not with a Screen margin. To keep the whole-pixel camera, write `subpixel_camera=0` in `settings.txt` while the launcher is closed. Android keeps the whole-pixel camera for now.
- **Key hints that follow your device.** The key hints at the bottom of the launcher's pages, the pause menu's included, now show only the keys of the device you used last: the keyboard's after a key, the controller's (D-pad, A, B, the menu button) after a button. Controls shows Escape and F2 to F11 only once a keyboard is used.
- **Display** now reads Profile, Quality, Window, View, HUD, Color correction; **Continuous transitions** moved to Display's Advanced page, with Core, Vsync, Neighbor workers, Scaling and Screen margin.
- On a screen where every Window choice comes to the same size, choosing Fill now starts the game fullscreen, as Display's diagram shows it.
- The neighbouring rooms fill in more reliably, under the sea in Ages in particular.

### Fixed

- In the drawn-back view (Medium and Far), far rooms are prepared a little before they come into view, so their corners show black much less often at the edges of the screen, swimming in particular.
- In Far, when Link first reaches the past in Ages, the rooms to his left, past the Maku sapling's grove, now fill in much faster instead of staying black. The same goes for the rooms beyond any other room that starts a scene as Link enters it.
- At each door, while the next room loads, the status bar keeps its look from play (full width, with the item hotkeys) instead of switching for a moment to its original, narrow form.
- After the season changes in Seasons (crossing into an area of another season, or with the Rod of Seasons), and after a game loads, a piece of the season's or era's banner no longer stays drawn over the scenery for a frame.
- Enemies of the room Link leaves no longer vanish at the edge of the game's own screen during the scroll: they stay where they were and fade out once it ends, as the ones of the room entered fade in.
- Walking diagonally or on stairs in Enhanced, Link no longer shakes a pixel back and forth against the screen. The fix in 1.1.0 was not enough; the camera now follows Link where the picture draws him.

- In Enhanced, the cutscenes that draw a picture over the whole screen (the story of the tower told by a worker in Ages, and other scenes of both games and the fan games) now show it framed, as the game draws it, instead of amid the scenery with its top in the status bar and a line of its text copied above it. The rooms these scenes play in stay in the wide view.

- **Mods now work on Android:** put each mod in its own folder in `Android/data/org.theoraclesproject/files/mods`, from a computer over USB (since Android 11, most file managers cannot open `Android/data`), then switch it on in the game's Mods page, as on a computer. ## 1.1.0 (2026-10-09)

- **Two cores.** The games run on SameBoy (Accurate) as well as mGBA's Game Boy core (Fast), behind one interface; Fast plays by default on Android. Display's Advanced page chooses the core.
- **Three views in the shape of the screen.** The widescreen view has three levels, Near, Medium and Far, in 16:9 and in 4:3, and the launcher has a 4:3 layout.
- **Quality.** Display's Quality sets the view and the core for the device in one choice, Low to Max, chosen by the device at the first opening; after a game that ran slowly, the launcher suggests a lighter one.
- **Neighbouring rooms.** Two background instances prepare the rooms around (Neighbor workers, in Display's Advanced page); a neighbour is rendered again only when what it reads has changed; the rooms of the past stay drawn across time warps; the view follows the screen shake and full fades, and keeps the neighbours' animations in step and their rooms when the colour correction changes.
- **Controllers.** Every controller is read, and controllers are opened again after a game.
- **Fixes.** A diagonal walk no longer wobbles Link in the widescreen view; vsync stays off on Android when it is set to auto; the launcher writes its report to a file on Android.
- Settings files and saves of 1.0.0 still load, and 1.1.0 installs over 1.0.0 on Android.
